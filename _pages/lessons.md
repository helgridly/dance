You probably got here from my [list of everything I know about how to DJ](/how). I can teach you anything on that page.

We can do this many ways: starting from a blank slate (while frontloading the fun!); or we can focus on building specific skills you're interested in; or we can do less structured feedback / advice / question / chitchat sessions.

If you'd like to work with me, I ask for a small piece of homework upfront. Once you've done the homework, reach out and we can schedule a time!

## Homework??!!

**Find 3 tracks that you like that are all within a 5bpm range of each other.**

You can use [this site](https://www.beatsperminuteonline.com/) to tap out the pulse while listening to a song and get the BPM.

### And if you want extra credit...

For DJing, you need to have your music on your computer, not on a streaming service. So the extra credit task is:

**Find a way to download the tracks to your computer.**

This will give you a jumpstart on the practical, organizational side of the work. If you're stumped, AI can give you advice. It's okay to get started here, and then find you have more questions than answers -- you can bring them to our first session.
